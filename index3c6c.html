Hello! I'm an undergraduate aerospace engineering honors student at the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, currently in my senior year. I grew up fascinated by aircraft, rockets, multirotors - anything that flies. I have pursued this passion through research projects into numerical modelling of composite material failure modes and solar power generation, and by leading MASA, Michigan's rocketry team, which aims to be the first student team to send a liquid bipropellant rocket past the Kármán line to space!

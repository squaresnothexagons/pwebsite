I’ve spent the past few years chasing my dream of helping put a rocket into orbit, and I continue to do so daily through my own technical ventures. Here, you’ll find a collection of my work spanning propulsion, research, and leadership. Beyond the projects themselves, I invite you to explore the ideas, motivations, and long-term vision that drive them forward.
